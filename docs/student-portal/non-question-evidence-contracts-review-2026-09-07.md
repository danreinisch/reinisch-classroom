# Non-Question Goal Evidence Contracts — Review Draft — 2026-09-07

## Status

**Review draft only.** This is not an approved change to IEP wording, goal math, or collection cadence.

This companion audit covers current Week 1 / Week 2 roster goals whose measurement is not a simple assignment-question Accuracy/Percent score and that currently have no Q1 progress check.

The audit found **20 such class-context goals across 13 students**.

The purpose is to identify the *kind of evidence event* ReinischClassroom should capture. Do not force generic multiple-choice correctness onto a goal merely to make a chart non-empty.

## Design rules suggested by the audit

1. **Evidence-event identity comes first.** Define what one dot means before building the input control.
2. **Question evidence is only for goals genuinely measured by questions/work products.**
3. **Observation goals should produce teacher-recorded opportunities, trials, counts, or rubric scores.**
4. **Composite goals may need a small checklist rather than one yes/no field.**
5. **Benchmark goals should respect benchmark cadence.** No weekly filler data.
6. **Objective-aware broad parent goals should capture evidence at the measurable child-objective level whenever possible.**
7. **Returned-for-revision writing samples may remain visible as attempt evidence, but should not silently become finalized progress.**
8. **The Student Portal should render these records through the same evidence timeline once the underlying event is legitimately captured.**

## Already represented examples

The audit also found four non-question class-context goals that already have at least one Q1 progress check. Those existing records demonstrate that the Observation Center / teacher-recorded path can coexist with assignment question evidence; the remaining work is to make the capture contracts consistent and intentional.
