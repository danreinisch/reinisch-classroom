StepWorker action

Do Not Stop at “Unsafe”

Situation

A liquid is spilled in a walkway.

Unsafe response

Step over it and leave it for someone else.

Safer response

Keep people away, use the site’s warning/reporting procedure, and clean it only if trained and equipped.

Report

State the exact location and immediate need.

Day 1 • Protect Money and Information

Cash, Cards, and Account Numbers Need Boundaries

Unsafe requestSafe response
“Tell me another worker’s register PIN.”Do not share it; contact the supervisor.
“Text me a photo of the customer’s card.”Do not photograph or send it; report the request.
“Leave the cash drawer open while you take a break.”Follow the secure-close and handoff procedure.
“Use your personal account to receive this work payment.”Stop and verify through the employer’s official process.

Never share passwords, PINs, full card numbers, account numbers, or customer financial information outside the approved process.

Day 2 • Read the Whole Sign

Color and Shape Are Clues—Words Confirm

  1. Stop long enough to notice the sign.
  2. Read the printed words when available.
  3. Identify the symbol or picture.
  4. Look at the location: door, machine, container, floor, or required-work area.
  5. Follow the workplace instruction or ask before entering/using the area.

Do not rely on color alone. Signs vary, and color-vision differences can make color-only identification unreliable.

Day 3 • Report Formula

WHAT + WHERE + DANGER + NEED

Clear report

“A worker slipped near the freezer entrance. They are on the floor and say their ankle hurts. The floor is still wet. We need the trained responder and someone to keep people out of this area.”

WHAT

What happened and who is affected?

WHERE

Exact area, room, aisle, entrance, or equipment.

DANGER

Is the hazard still active?

NEED

What immediate help is required?

Day 3 • Do Not Make It Worse

Help Within Your Training

Do

  • Use the workplace emergency signal/contact.
  • Give the exact location.
  • Warn others away when safe.
  • Follow trained first-aid or evacuation directions.
  • Stay available to answer factual questions.

Do not

  • Move an injured person unless immediate danger or trained direction requires it.
  • Touch unknown substances.
  • Re-enter an unsafe area for belongings.
  • Take or share photos of an injured person.
  • Invent details you did not see.
