Day 1 • Learn

The Word Swap & the Tone Check

Friend-voice says…Work-voice says…
“Yeah, whatever”“Sure, I can do that”
“Huh?”“Could you say that once more?”
“That’s not my job”“I haven’t been trained on that yet — want me to grab someone who has?”
“My bad lol”“I’m sorry about that — I’ll fix it”
(silence, walks off)“Excuse me — I’m heading to break, back at 2:15”

The tone check — three dials

Volume — indoor, steady; not a mumble, not a broadcast. Speed — slow enough to land; rushed words sound annoyed even when they aren’t. Face & body — eyes up, phone down; tone leaks through posture. Plus the four anchors that work everywhere: please, thank you, excuse me, people’s names.

The professional voice isn’t fake — it’s a uniform for your words. You already own several voices; work just gets the crisp one. And names are the cheat code: “Thanks, Maria” lands twice as warm as “thanks.”

Day 1 • You Try It

Swap It, Tone It

The manager asks you to redo a display you just finished. Friend-voice wants to say “Seriously??” Work-voice says…
The swap keeps your dignity AND gets you the info that prevents a third redo. Sighs and slammed shelves are tone too — the loudest kind.
A coworker mumbles “thanks I guess” while staring at their phone. Which dial is ruining perfectly polite WORDS?
“I guess” plus a phone-stare reads as sarcasm no matter the script. The dials can cancel the words — which is why we check all three.
You’re asked to do something you truly don’t know how to do. The respectful, honest line is…
“Yet” is the magic word — honest about now, open about later. (Sound like next week’s topic? It is. Asking for help gets a whole week.)
Take the Week 12 late call — “name + situation + arrival + sorry for the trouble” — and imagine it delivered with all three dials wrong. Same words. What happens?
Think first — then click to reveal
Mumbled, rushed, annoyed — and a responsible call sounds like an excuse. The manager hears the DIALS, not the script. Every script you’ve learned this year is only as good as the voice carrying it — which is why this short week exists.

Day 1 • Do

Tone Studio

Your task Partner rounds: draw a friend-voice card (“huh?”, “whatever”, “my bad”, “not my job”…), perform the work-voice swap with all three dials right — partner scores volume / speed / face on the rubric. Then re-run ONE of your old scripts (change check, late call, or say-it-back) in full professional voice.

Before you leave: Perform your best swap for me — dials and all.
