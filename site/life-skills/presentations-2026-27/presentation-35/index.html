CategoryMeaningSample

Ask What the Expense Does

Phone service

May be a need when required for transportation, work schedules, safety, or support communication. Optional upgrades may be wants.

Clothing

Required work shoes may be a need. An additional style purchase may be personal spending.

Useful question: What happens in this scenario if the expense is not paid or purchased now?

Change a Category, Not the Paycheck

Possible correction A

Reduce personal spending from $85 to $60.
New total: $300

New total: $300

Protect Needs and Make a Choice

OptionChangeResult
APersonal spending $70 → $50Transportation increase covered
BSavings $50 → $40; personal $70 → $60Increase covered across two categories
CIgnore the $20 increasePlan becomes inaccurate
DSpend $20 more than net payPlan becomes over budget

The best adjustment depends on the scenario and available choices. The mathematical requirement is that the revised plan still fits.
