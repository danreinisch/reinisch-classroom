Three Different Messages

Absence, Lateness, or Request?

Absence

You cannot attend the scheduled shift.

Lateness

You expect to attend but cannot arrive on time.

Schedule-change request

You ask in advance to change a future shift. The request may be approved or denied.

Emergency

Safety and urgent help come first; communicate with work as soon as reasonably possible.

Day 1 • Act Early

Do Not Wait for the Shift to Start

If you know now

Contact the correct person now. Early notice gives the workplace more time to respond.

If the situation changes

Give an updated arrival estimate or confirm that you cannot attend. Do not disappear after the first message.

Your shift begins at 4:00 PM. At 2:30 you learn your approved ride is unavailable. When should you begin the backup and contact process?
Use the earliest useful time
At 2:30 PM. Do not wait until 4:00 to begin responding.

Day 2 • The Script

Brief and Complete

Sample call-in

“Hello, this is Maya Chen. I am scheduled today from 3:00 to 7:00 PM. I am ill and cannot safely attend my shift. I am following the call-in procedure now. Please let me know whether I need to complete another step before my next scheduled shift.”

Included: name, shift, inability to attend, reason category, and follow-up. No detailed medical story is needed for this practice.

Day 3 • A Request Is Not Approval

Keep the Original Schedule Until Confirmed

Request

“May I move my Thursday 4:00–8:00 shift to Friday 4:00–8:00 because of an appointment?”

Approval

The supervisor or scheduling system confirms the change. Until then, the original Thursday shift still applies.

Do not: trade shifts informally, assume silence means yes, or skip the original shift without confirmation.

Day 3 • Message Pattern

Current Shift + Conflict + Request

Sample request

“This is Jordan. I am scheduled Thursday from 4:00 to 8:00 PM. I have an appointment that cannot be moved. May I request Friday from 4:00 to 8:00 PM instead? I understand the change needs approval, and I will follow the current schedule unless I receive confirmation.”

Offer only times and transportation you can actually manage.
