Low-Setup Materials

Everything Fits on One Table

Needed

  • Printed item/price cards
  • Fake money
  • Calculator when appropriate
  • Simple receipt slips
  • Cashier and customer role cards

Not needed

  • Real products or food
  • Multiple stations
  • Real cash or cards
  • Elaborate decorations
  • A loud whole-class role-play
Run one transaction at a time. Students may practice with the teacher, a trusted partner, or independently using role cards.

Day 3 • Practice Rules

Accuracy Before Theater

Do

  • Preview the role card.
  • Use fake money only.
  • Pause for calculator or checklist use.
  • Ask for help instead of guessing.
  • Complete one transaction fully.

Avoid

  • Surprise complicated totals.
  • Speed contests.
  • Real personal payment information.
  • Adding upset-customer conflict unless selected.
  • Correcting every step during performance.
