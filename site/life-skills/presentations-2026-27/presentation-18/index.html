Day 1 • Do

Four Stations

Your task Rotate through all four; each has a card to complete. Station 1 — The Posting: a new job posting; decode pay, hours, requirements, and give the fit verdict with one reason. Station 2 — The Application: a half-finished application seeded with five classic errors; run the four-pass sweep, fix what’s fixable, N/A what needs it. Station 3 — The Workplace: three rapid scenarios (a blunt correction, a gossip offer, a multistep direction given aloud once) — write or say the right move for each. Station 4 — The Money: count a mixed handful hairy-money style, then answer one enough-money question with tax padding.

Check out: Your four completed station cards are the grade — and the proof of the semester.

The story so far

Semester one, complete: you can find and judge a job, fill its application safely and perfectly, handle its money, show up right, do the work, and handle its people — managers AND coworkers. Four stations proved it in one period. That’s not review; that’s a résumé waiting to happen.
