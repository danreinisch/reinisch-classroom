Capstone Rules

Use Fictional Information Safely

Use

  • Teacher-created job postings
  • Fictional worker information
  • Sample schedules/paychecks
  • Fake money
  • Approved calculator and checklists

Do not use

  • Real Social Security numbers
  • Real bank/card/account information
  • Unnecessary student medical details
  • Real cash
  • Unapproved employer contact

Day 1 • Fit Decision

FIT, NOT FIT, or NEED MORE INFORMATION

FIT

Duties, schedule, transportation, and requirements align with the fictional worker’s information.

NOT FIT

A confirmed factor makes the job unrealistic or unsafe.

NEED MORE INFORMATION

A key support, duty, schedule, or transportation question remains unanswered.

Evidence

Use at least one verified factor.

Day 1 • Money Check

Wage Is One Part of Fit

hours × wage = expected gross

Sample: 12 hours × $13/hour = $156 expected gross. If transportation costs $18, a simple gross-minus-travel comparison is $138 before deductions and other costs.

The capstone uses only provided amounts. Do not invent deductions or exact take-home pay.
