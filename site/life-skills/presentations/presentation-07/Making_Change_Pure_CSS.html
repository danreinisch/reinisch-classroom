📖 Key Vocabulary

Adding Money
Putting amounts together to find a total. Example: $2 + $3 = $5
Subtracting Money
Taking one amount away from another. Example: $5 - $2 = $3
Total
The final amount when you add. What you have to pay.
Change
Money you get back when you pay more than something costs.

➕ Adding Money: Trick #1

Add Dollars First, Then Cents

Break it into two easy steps instead of one hard step!

Problem: $4.50 + $3.25 = ?

Step 1: Add the dollars → $4 + $3 = $7

Step 2: Add the cents → 50¢ + 25¢ = 75¢

Put together: $7 + 75¢ = $7.75

➕ Adding Money: Trick #2

Look for "Friendly Numbers"

Some cents add up to $1.00 - look for these!

Amount 1+ Amount 2= Total
75¢25¢$1.00
50¢50¢$1.00
60¢40¢$1.00
90¢10¢$1.00

Example: $2.75 + $1.25 → 75 + 25 = $1! So: $2 + $1 + $1 = $4.00

➖ Subtracting: The Count Up Method

Count Up - The Easiest Way!

Instead of subtracting, count UP from the smaller number to the bigger number!

Problem: $5.00 - $3.00 = ?

Think: Start at $3.00, count up to $5.00

Count: "$3... $4... $5" → That's 2 dollars you counted!

Answer: $5.00 - $3.00 = $2.00

💡 Why Count Up Works

  • No borrowing or carrying needed
  • This is how cashiers actually do it!

➖ Subtracting: Round & Adjust

For .99 Prices - Round Up!

Prices ending in .99 are almost the next dollar. Use that!

Problem: $10.00 - $3.99 = ?

Think: $3.99 is almost $4.00

Easy math: $10.00 - $4.00 = $6.00

Adjust: We subtracted 1¢ too much, so add it back

Answer: $6.00 + 1¢ = $6.01

🎯 Making Change: The Full Process

Now we put it all together! Learn the complete step-by-step process for making change like a cashier.

❓ WHAT

Give back exact coins and bills when someone pays more.

💡 WHY

This is a real job skill!

📝 HOW

Count up with coins first, then bills.

📋 The 5 Steps (Part 1)

1
Say the Price Out Loud

"Your total is $3.67"

2
Add Pennies to Get to a "5" or "0"

"$3.67... $3.68, $3.69, $3.70" (3 pennies)

3
Add Coins to Reach the Next Dollar

"$3.70... $3.75 (nickel)... $4.00 (quarter)"

📋 The 5 Steps (Part 2)

4
Add Dollar Bills to Reach the Payment

"$4.00... $5.00 (one dollar)"

5
Hand Over the Change

The coins and bills you counted ARE the change!

📌 Full Example

Price: $6.42 | Payment: $10.00

• "$6.42, $6.43, $6.44, $6.45" (3 pennies)

• "$6.50 (nickel), $6.75 (quarter), $7.00 (quarter)"

• "$8, $9, $10" (3 one-dollar bills)

Change: 3 pennies + nickel + 2 quarters + $3 = $3.58

🎮 Practice #1

Price: $2.75 | Payment: $3.00
What change?
✓ Correct! $3.00 - $2.75 = 25¢ = 1 quarter
✗ Count up from $2.75 to $3.00

🎮 Practice #2

Price: $7.89 | Payment: $10.00

✓ Correct! $10.00 - $7.89 = $2.11
✗ Count up from $7.89 to $10.00
