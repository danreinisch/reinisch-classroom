Career Overview

What is this career?

Interior Design is the art and science of refining a room or building to make it more functional and aesthetically pleasing — transforming an ordinary space into a thoughtfully designed home.

Daily Responsibilities
  • Work directly with clients to understand their vision
  • Draft and present detailed design plans
  • Source materials, furniture, and finishes
  • Oversee contractors to bring the vision to life
Work Environment

No two days are the same — you might be sketching in a creative studio, planning from an office, or walking through an active job site.

Education & Training

Degree Required

Most interior designers earn a Bachelor's degree in Interior Design or a related field such as architecture. Programs typically take 4 years and cover design theory, drafting, CAD software, and project management.
