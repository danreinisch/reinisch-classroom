Side Projects / Outside Work (Only if asked)

Compass + Guardrails

Anchor sentence
“I build reliable, scalable systems in ambiguous environments and communicate clearly while doing it.”
Every answer should quietly prove this.
Three themes (what feedback will score)
Technical Judgment
  • Designs for scale, failure, and change
  • Explains why choices were made
  • Acknowledges tradeoffs without prompting
Collaboration
  • Works across teams without ego
  • Handles disagreement calmly
  • Shares ownership instead of territory
Ownership
  • Takes responsibility beyond “my code”
  • Spots problems before tickets exist
  • Improves systems, not just features
Behavioral cheat code
Context → Decision → Outcome → Learning (Learning = senior signal.)
Language to avoid
Delete: “I have no idea”, “not sure”, “maybe”, “I guess”. Replace with: “Based on what I know…” + one smart assumption + one question back.
