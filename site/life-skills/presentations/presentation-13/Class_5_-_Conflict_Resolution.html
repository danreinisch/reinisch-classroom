How to Dialogue through Successful Conflict Resolution

What is conflict?

A disagreement between two or more people.

Conflict is a normal occurrence and part of any workplace.

The results of conflict may include:

Lowering team morale tap to learn more
When conflict goes unresolved, it creates tension that spreads across the team, making people feel uncomfortable and disengaged at work.
Decreased productivity tap to learn more
People spend energy avoiding or worrying about conflict instead of focusing on their tasks, which slows everything down.
Increase in absenteeism tap to learn more
Workers may start calling out or avoiding work altogether to escape the stressful environment caused by ongoing conflict.

How to Resolve Conflict

Conflict Resolution Rules

1

Agree to resolve the conflict.

2

Take turns talking and do not interrupt.

3

No name calling.

4

Be truthful about what is bothering you, and state it clearly.

5

Listen to the other person, and be sure you understand, how they understand the problem.

6

Be willing to compromise.

Resolving conflict with "I" statements

I feel _____ when you _____. Can you please _____ then I/we can _____.
Example

I feel hurt when you ignore me. Can you please acknowledge me when I speak to you, then we can get along better.

Role Play Scenarios

1
Scenario 1

You put a soda in the work room refrigerator and someone steals it and drinks it. You are now confronting the person whom you suspect.

2
Scenario 2

Someone is spreading gossip about you at work by saying that you are lazy and take naps in your car when you are supposed to be working. You decide to confront that person.

3
Scenario 3

Your co-worker parks too close to your car and leaves a dent in your door. How do you discuss this with them?

How would you respond or react? What would you say to that person? How can you diffuse the situation without causing a bigger conflict?

Tower Challenge:

The goal of the exercise is to have your team build the tallest freestanding tower with 3x5 cards.

Rules:

  1. You will be given 5 minutes to plan and build your tower.
  2. You will have 7 minutes for the actual construction of the tower.
  3. While you plan, you may not damage the cards in any way, but you may talk to your team.
  4. While you build the tower, you may not speak to each other.

Details

Materials: 50 3x5 cards (or deck of playing cards).
Group Size: 4-6 people
5 min planning + 7 min building
