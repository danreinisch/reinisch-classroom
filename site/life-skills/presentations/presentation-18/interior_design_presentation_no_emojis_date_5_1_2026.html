Interior design combines creativity and planning to shape rooms and buildings into spaces that are both beautiful and functional.

Career Overview

What is this career?

Interior Design is the art and science of refining a room or building to enhance a more functional and aesthetically pleasing home.

What are the daily Responsibilities of an Interior Designer?

Interior Designers must work with clients, Draft design plans, Source materials, and oversee contractors to bring a cohesive vision alive.

What does the work environment look like for an Interior Designer?

The work environment varies from a creative studio, office, or active on-site visits.

Education and Training

What degree or training is required to become an Interior Designer?

To become an interior designer, you typically need a bachelor’s degree in interior design or a related field, such as architecture.

What is the monthly income?

The average hourly rate for an interior designer is $30.52. If you multiply that by 40 (average working hours per week), that is $1,220.80. Then, if you take that and multiply it by it by 52, you end up with $63,481.60. Then, you can divide that by 12 to learn the average monthly income…which is $5,290.13. Doing it this way avoids issues with differences in number of weeks per month.
