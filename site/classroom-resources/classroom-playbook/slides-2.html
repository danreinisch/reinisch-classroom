Directions + help

Listen → ask → start.

You do not need to understand everything instantly. You do need to listen first, ask about the part that is unclear, and then take the first step.

The usual learning loop

Most class periods follow a familiar rhythm.

Not every day uses every step. This is the basic routine so you usually know what is coming next. We do not need to reinvent school from scratch every hour.

Technology without the drama

Use the Chromebook for class. Keep personal devices out of it.

Your charged Chromebook is already part of being ready. Once class starts, use it for what we are doing and follow the school rules for personal electronics, headphones, recording, and AI.

Missed something?

Being absent creates a gap — not a catastrophe.

If you miss class, catching up is your responsibility. I will make that as straightforward as I can: check ReinischClassroom, see what you missed, ask what still is not clear, and make a plan.
