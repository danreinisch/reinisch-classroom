The short version

Five rules cover almost everything.

Expectation 1

Respect the person. Fair does not always mean identical.

Nobody gets to make this room harder for somebody else. The expectations are shared; the support may be different because different people need different things to do the work successfully.

Expectation 2

Ready means ready enough to start.

I do not expect military precision. I do expect you to come in ready enough to begin — and to solve small problems without letting them eat the whole period.

Expectation 3

Participation is not a volume contest.

You do not have to talk the most or pretend to love every activity. You do have to engage with the work in some real way.
