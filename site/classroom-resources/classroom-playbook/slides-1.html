Before the rules: the point

Expectations should make class easier to navigate — not harder to survive.

The goal is simple: protect time, dignity, and everyone's chance to learn.

When expectations are clear

The short version

Five rules cover almost everything.

I am not looking for perfection. I am looking for effort, communication, and a willingness to rejoin when something goes wrong.

Expectation 1

Respect is not “everybody has to like everybody.”

It means nobody gets to make this room harder for somebody else. Protect people's dignity, space, work, and privacy.

A rule worth knowing

Fair is not everybody getting the same thing.

Fair means everybody gets a real shot at learning. The expectations are shared. The support may be different.

Shared goal

Make progress and become more independent

Everyone is expected to learn, participate, and grow — even if the work does not look identical for every student.

Different support

Use what helps you do the work

That might be extra time, examples, sentence frames, read-aloud support, guided notes, a different response format, or another approved support.

Expectation 2

Ready means ready enough to start.

I do not expect military precision. I do expect you to come in ready enough to begin — and to solve small problems without letting them eat the whole period.
