Wrap-Up

Week 13 Quick Reference

The story so far

The battle turns preparation into a real test. Craft choices—delay, sudden reversal, confinement, and rapid commands—force Alex to improvise. His leadership is imperfect, but earlier lessons in delegation, cooperation, and care give the alliance a way to keep responding.

Keep asking

  • What happens after the final strike against Alex?
  • Can the alliance reach the inner settlement?
