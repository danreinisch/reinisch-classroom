Escape Is Not the Same as Safety

The travelers

Alex, Sekun-ak, and Monda-ak have survived captivity, a river, hunger, and a storm.

The destination

The warning

Denta-ah has expanded its weapons, labor system, and regional ambition.

The new question

What does Alex owe the community once he carries that knowledge home?

Wrap-Up

Week 9 Quick Reference

The story so far

The final journey obstacle tests trust, but reaching Winten-ah creates a larger challenge. Alex’s warning makes him responsible for helping the community act, while the preparation plan shows that survival and values must be defended together.

Keep asking

  • Can Alex lead people from several tribes as one force?
  • Will the new tools work under real pressure?
  • What will leadership cost Alex and his relationships?
