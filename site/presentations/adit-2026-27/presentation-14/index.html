Wrap-Up

Week 14 Quick Reference

The story so far

The battle ends, but responsibility expands. Alex must manage anger during the final clash, Douglas faces consequences while fleeing, and Sekun-ak turns victory toward burial, healing, promises, and a new community that includes former enemies.

Keep asking

  • How will the alliance remember both victory and loss?
  • What will “home” mean when Alex returns to Winten-ah?
  • Can one journey end without creating another duty?
