Wrap-Up

Week 10 Quick Reference

The story so far

Alex earns credibility by pairing skill with restraint, then distributes leadership so the alliance can act as a system. Janta-ak’s advance team shows that preparation is not only tactical: its purpose is measured by how leaders respond to vulnerable people.

Keep asking

  • Will the alliance’s training hold under unfamiliar danger?
  • Can the advance team complete its risky plan?
  • How will loss change the meaning of leadership?
