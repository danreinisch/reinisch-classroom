Two Outsiders Choose Different Roles

Wrap-Up

Week 8 Quick Reference

The story so far

Escape does not end the conflict; it changes its form. Across field, forest, river, storm, and cave, survival depends on reading the setting and combining Alex’s training, Sekun-ak’s local expertise, and Monda-ak’s strength and loyalty.

Keep asking

  • Can the group find its way back to Winten-ah?
  • What warning must they carry home?
  • How will Douglas and Denta-ah respond to the escape?
