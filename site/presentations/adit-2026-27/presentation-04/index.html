Day 1 • ELA Concept

Theme Grows Through Repeated Care

Dan helps Alex reach safety.

Care begins before Alex can ask clearly for it.

Niten-eh and Malen-eh remain with him.

Belonging is shown through sustained action.

The community replaces ruined clothing.

Recovery includes restoring a place in daily life.

Alex asks why they help him.

He notices the gap between being called an outsider and being treated as one of them.

Today’s bridge: A theme is not a single word. It is an idea the text develops through connected choices and consequences.

Wrap-Up

Week 4 Quick Reference

The story so far

Recovery teaches Alex that belonging is built through care. He returns that care to Monda-ak, accepts a lasting oath, and turns responsibility into a new quest: earn a role that lets him contribute to the community.

Keep asking

  • What future cost will Banda-ak’s oath carry?
  • Will Sekun-ak’s tasks test skill fairly—or Alex personally?
  • Can Alex learn to balance courage with judgment?
