Wrap-Up

Week 6 Quick Reference

The story so far

Alex releases a symbol of his former world, grows into the role of Manta-ak, and builds years of work and relationship inside Winten-ah. The stama crisis then shows that identity is not replacement: knowledge from Alex Hawk now serves Manta-ak’s community.

Keep asking

  • What makes technology “stama” rather than merely a tool?
  • Who is the rumored outsider?
  • Will Alex’s two-world perspective prevent conflict or place him inside it?
