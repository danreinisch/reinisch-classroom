Wrap-Up

Week 15 Quick Reference

The story so far

The novel closes one conflict and opens another. Ceremony begins recovery, the return to Winten-ah reveals new loss, and the life oath reconnects Alex’s earliest act of responsibility to a new mission. Home is no longer one place; it is a network of duties and relationships.

Keep asking

  • Can a person fully choose between two genuine homes?
  • Which duty should Alex answer first—and why?
  • How does the oath turn the ending into the beginning of the next book?

This completes A Door into Time. The next novel continues Alex’s unfinished journey.
