Wrap-Up

Week 12 Quick Reference

The story so far

Liberation becomes possible through many linked choices: Janta-ak protects and grows a hidden network, Alex’s force frees work crews, and workers seize a brief gap in Denta-ah’s control. No single hero opens the way; cooperation does.

Keep asking

  • Why has Denta-ah withdrawn from the outer area?
  • Is the open gate an opportunity or part of a trap?
  • Can the alliance preserve cooperation under battlefield pressure?
