Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop perspective, leadership, and ethical choice. We examine how danger looks from the marching army, the advance team, and the children—and how each perspective reveals a different cost.

I can…

  • Compare how multiple perspectives frame the same mission.
  • Explain how loss changes leadership decisions.
  • Evaluate choices made when every option carries risk.

Wrap-Up

Week 11 Quick Reference

The story so far

The mission’s cost appears from three directions: the main army faces dangers no training can fully predict, the advance team accepts personal loss, and the children wait with little control over the outcome. Leadership means carrying all three realities at once.

Keep asking

  • What has Janta-ak’s team been asked to do inside Denta-ah?
  • Can the main force protect people without losing its purpose?
  • How will survivors and forced workers shape the coming conflict?
