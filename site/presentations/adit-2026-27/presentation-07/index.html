Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Introduce conflict, setting, and cause-and-effect analysis. We examine how defensive settings, new weapons, missing information, and unequal power change what the travelers understand—and what choices remain possible.

I can…

  • Explain how setting changes the possibilities in a conflict.
  • Trace causes and consequences across connected chapters.
  • Evaluate how new evidence changes a character’s moral responsibility.

Stipa-ah Provides Cause-and-Effect Evidence

Some buildings are damaged from above.

Ordinary hand-to-hand attack does not explain the pattern.

Most residents are missing.

The event includes forced movement, not only destruction.

Food stores remain.

The people did not simply relocate normally.

The travelers bury the dead and continue.

Moral duty delays but does not end the mission.

Today’s bridge: Evidence becomes meaningful when readers ask what cause could reasonably produce the visible effect.
