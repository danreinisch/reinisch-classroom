Point of View Withholds Crucial Information

The reader shares his incomplete view.

The trail turns beyond a rise.

Wrap-Up

Week 10 Quick Reference

The story so far

The ambush, the vast arrival, and the ship negotiation repeatedly place Alex inside incomplete information. Resilience means recovering from embarrassment, revising pace, listening to other perspectives, and solving the next problem after expectations fail.

Keep asking

  • What conditions did Alex fail to negotiate for aboard the ship?
  • Will Versa-eh accept the arranged marriage?
  • What dangers operate on Okrent-ah?
