Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Introduce decision, setting, and cause-and-effect analysis. We trace how a request for help, a dangerous hunt, and a medical emergency test responsibility and judgment.

I can…

  • Identify a decision and the pressures surrounding it.
  • Trace causes and effects across a dangerous sequence.
  • Explain how responsibility develops through action and consequence.

Wrap-Up

Week 7 Quick Reference

The story so far

The hunt begins as a service bargain, becomes an exercise in adaptation, and ends as a medical emergency. Alex’s responsibility is measured not by making a perfect first choice, but by gathering evidence, revising plans, and staying accountable for consequences.

Keep asking

  • How long will Werda-ak need to recover?
  • Will the team leave a member behind to protect the mission’s speed?
  • What new conditions wait beyond the lake?
