Wrap-Up

Week 5 Quick Reference

The story so far

The Tonton-ah ambush proves the kidnappers planned far ahead, but their failure yields the name Lusta-ah. New testimony, map reasoning, horses, healing, and the team’s growing restraint transform a hidden threat into a larger but more possible journey.

Keep asking

  • Is Lusta-ah truly in the blank region?
  • How far can the horses carry the group before winter?
  • What kind of guide could help them choose among unknown paths?
