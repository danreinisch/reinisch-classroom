Wrap-Up

Week 9 Quick Reference

The story so far

Spring renews the travelers and reopens the route, but movement does not remove uncertainty. The bear encounter rewards vigilance, while the trade for faster horses shows how urgency can make visible benefits outweigh questions that still need answers.

Keep asking

  • What will Versa-eh choose when given an opportunity?
  • Will the faster horses prove worth the trade?
  • How will the nearby village respond?
