Wrap-Up

Week 12 Quick Reference

The story so far

Rescue creates access to a new source. Harta-ak’s observation confirms Lanta-eh’s destination, his history explains resistance to Draka-ak, and the traka-ta network makes the kidnapping’s timing plausible. Evidence replaces an impossible mystery with a difficult but clearer problem.

Keep asking

  • Who first reported Lanta-eh’s location?
  • Can the group enter a guarded city without direct attack?
  • How will Draka-ak respond if he expects a rescue?
