Day 3 • ELA Concept

A Precise Answer Can Still Mislead

Alex asks for the most direct route.

The question prioritizes distance only.

Tokin-ak answers “left.”

The response is accurate within those terms.

The route reaches a vast lake.

A hidden condition changes the usefulness of the answer.

The other path would avoid the lake.

A fuller question would have revealed the tradeoff.

Today’s bridge: Information is not wisdom by itself. Decision quality depends on the question, priorities, assumptions, and consequences considered.

Wrap-Up

Week 6 Quick Reference

The story so far

Tokin-ak transforms the group’s idea of a guide. His listening detects danger and his experience broadens their knowledge, but the fork shows that guidance cannot replace precise questions and responsible choice. Even the wrong-feeling route may contain an unexpected possibility.

Keep asking

  • What condition comes with the ship crossing?
  • Did the kidnappers take the longer southern route?
  • How will the team decide when guidance and urgency conflict?
