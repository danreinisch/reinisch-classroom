Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Apply evidence-based character analysis. We use choices during danger, confinement, and negotiation to evaluate courage and trace how distrust becomes earned cooperation.

I can…

  • Support a character inference with relevant evidence.
  • Trace how a relationship changes across conflict.
  • Evaluate courage as action shaped by purpose and consequence.

Week 3 Quick Reference

The story so far

Captivity begins with anger over the bridge, but new evidence reveals a more complicated conflict. Alex’s protective courage, Rinka-ak’s honesty, and both characters’ willingness to honor a risky bargain transform distrust into cooperation.
