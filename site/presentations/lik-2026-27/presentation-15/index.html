Wrap-Up

Week 15 Quick Reference

The story so far

Grief first removes Alex from leadership, then returns as rage before slowly becoming part of the journey home. Senta-eh’s care, the group’s shared mourning, and Winten-ah’s complicated welcome show that justice can restore Lanta-eh without undoing loss.

Keep asking

  • Will Dan regain enough strength to reach the door?
  • Which home will Alex ultimately choose?
  • Has the mission changed what “home” means?
