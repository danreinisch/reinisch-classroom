Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop evidence-based character analysis. We compare actions, decisions, and consequences to infer how three travelers approach danger, responsibility, and problem solving.

I can…

  • Identify specific evidence from a dangerous sequence.
  • Infer a character trait from action and consequence.
  • Compare how different strengths contribute to one solution.

Wrap-Up

Week 2 Quick Reference

The story so far

The Kranda-ah sequence shows three different responses to danger: Werda-ak acts boldly, Alex calculates and improvises, and Senta-eh supplies strength and perspective. Their solution works, but its consequences prove that practical ingenuity never removes responsibility.

Keep asking

  • Who owns the destroyed bridge?
  • Can the group repair trust with the nearby village?
  • What danger produced Werda-ak’s scream?
