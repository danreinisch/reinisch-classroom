Wrap-Up

Week 13 Quick Reference

The story so far

The group cannot match Lasta-ah in numbers, defenses, or technology. It therefore depends on allies, reconnaissance, patience, timing, and the city’s own traka-ta system. The strategy demonstrates courage and ingenuity while also revealing the limits of control.

Keep asking

  • Is Lanta-eh really on the third floor?
  • Can the group escape after sealing itself inside?
  • What consequences will the citywide fires create?
