Wrap-Up

Week 8 Quick Reference

The story so far

Matori-ah and Hakun-ah show that progress is not measured only in miles. Recovery, preparation, honest communication, and shelter protect the people who make the mission possible, while winter teaches Alex to accept patience as a responsible choice.

Keep asking

  • How will the winter change the travelers?
  • Will the thaw arrive when expected?
  • What dangers wait on the eastern side of the mountains?
