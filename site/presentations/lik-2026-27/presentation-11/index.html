What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop suspense and problem-solving analysis. We trace how observation, uncertainty, shipwreck, and island survival create escalating problems that require shared action.

  • Trace how one problem produces the next.
  • Explain why shared expertise improves survival.

Wrap-Up

Week 11 Quick Reference

The story so far

The voyage and shipwreck turn observation into a survival tool. Werda-ak notices a possible clue, Alex reads the pursuing ship, sailors contribute specialized knowledge, and castaways organize labor. Shared problem solving does not remove danger, but it repeatedly creates the next chance.

Keep asking

  • Who is aboard the approaching rescue vessel?
  • Will the castaways reach the mainland?
  • How much farther is Lasta-ah after the detour?
