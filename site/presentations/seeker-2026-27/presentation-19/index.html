Specific

Explainable

Representative

Foundation — 4–6 sentences

Use one detail and frames: “One important event is ___.” “This changes ___ because ___.” “This shows ___.”

Supported — one developed paragraph

Wrap-Up

Week 19 Quick Reference

The story so far

Seeker begins with coercion, secrecy, and fear of transformation. Across the novel, Kagan and Ella learn that neither enhancement nor intelligence guarantees wisdom. Trust, ethical choice, and the courage to revise redirect power from extermination toward consent—and allow the final chapter to imagine hope as both joy and responsibility.

Keep asking

  • Am I stating a complete theme rather than a topic?
  • Does my evidence cover beginning, middle, and end?
  • Have I explained how the message develops—not just what happens?
