Risk Cannot Be Removed

Stay put

Avoids immediate movement but gives up possible new evidence.

Move toward the center

Creates danger but may restore sensors or reveal a pattern.

Search randomly

Offers little confidence but preserves a chance.

Gather food and water

Survival preparation supports whatever strategy follows.

Objective Summary vs. Interpretation

Type of writingIts job
SummaryThe allied group chooses to move toward the expected center and stop at a pond for supplies.
InterpretationTheir choice suggests action may be necessary even when knowledge is incomplete.
Summary testOnly essential events; neutral language; no judgment.
Theme testA broader message supported by the event.

Keep summary and analysis distinct so the reader can see where the novel ends and your reasoning begins.

Wrap-Up

Week 9 Quick Reference

The story so far

New information reveals that the search may be Seeker’s design, but it does not reveal the purpose. The group must act anyway. At the pond, choices about an animal, an alliance, and an ambush show that uncertainty limits control without eliminating moral responsibility.

Keep asking

  • Am I stating a theme or summarizing plot?
  • What new evidence changes the characters’ assumptions?
  • Is my summary neutral, concise, and complete enough to follow?
