Day 1 • ELA Concept

Diction Changes the Frame

“Contest”

Seeker’s neutral label suggests rules and evaluation.

“Set us up”

Assigns agency and blame to Seeker.

“Gladiator entertainment”

Recasts the event as spectacle and cruelty.

“Arbitrary” and “preposterous”

Reject the logic, not just the outcome.

Today’s bridge: When characters rename an event, they compete to control how it will be understood.

Wrap-Up

Week 15 Quick Reference

The story so far

Kagan and Ella convert trauma into questions. Their dialogue forces Seeker to reveal information, but superior-intellect claims block persuasion. Unable to change Seeker’s plan, they turn to Ory and accept an almost impossible sacrifice.

Keep asking

  • What goal drives each speaker’s language?
  • How do questions escalate across the exchange?
  • What is the cumulative impact of several craft choices?
