Revise an Earlier Interpretation

PartWhat it must do

New evidenceUse a decisive detail from Chapters 52–54.

Wrap-Up

Week 18 Quick Reference

The story so far

Ella’s final, testable contradiction exposes Seeker’s compromised memory. The reboot confirms manipulation, ends the contest, and prevents extermination. Repair remains incomplete, but the novel replaces forced selection with consent, representation, and a more hopeful—yet accountable—view of humanity.

Keep asking

  • What exact evidence forces revision?
  • Which earlier interpretation changes, and which part remains?
  • What responsibility follows after truth is recognized?
