Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Introduce evaluation of perspectives. We identify what each character wants, which assumptions guide that goal, and how evidence strengthens or weakens the perspective.

I can…

  • Identify a speaker’s goal and assumptions.
  • Separate a promise from evidence that it will be kept.
  • Compare perspectives using specific details and reasonable inference.

Promise, Evidence, Assumption

Type of claimEvaluation question
PromiseWinners receive access to Seeker’s technology.
EvidenceSeeker sends Ory a large propulsion file.
AssumptionThe file is accurate and potentially exploitable.
ComplicationSeeker may understand their hidden goal.

Do not treat a confident statement as proof; ask what independent detail supports it.

Wrap-Up

Week 16 Quick Reference

The story so far

A technical exchange hides an attack plan, Seeker reveals a more powerful source behind its story, and an Israeli commander weighs mercy against national survival. Across all three, decisions depend on promises, incomplete evidence, and the willingness to test assumptions.

Keep asking

  • What does each perspective want?
  • Which claim is a promise, and which detail is evidence?
  • What new information should cause a reasonable revision?
