What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop evaluation of perspectives. We compare reasoning, detect bias and blind spots, and examine how new evidence can expose manipulation.

I can…

  • Distinguish a perspective’s claim from its evidence.
  • Identify bias, blind spots, and manipulation.
  • Compare competing explanations and revise an interpretation.

Reasoning taskKey question

Wrap-Up

Week 17 Quick Reference

The story so far

Seeker offers a sweeping explanation of challenge and purpose, but it cannot justify the immediate slaughter. Ella treats contradiction as evidence, then reverses Seeker’s intelligence hierarchy to argue that the ASI has manipulated the probe from within.

Keep asking

  • Does the reasoning answer the actual objection?
  • Which perspective benefits from its own assumptions?
  • Which interpretation explains the greatest number of details?
