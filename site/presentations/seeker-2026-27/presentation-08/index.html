Trace and Complicate a Theme

MoveWhat to show
PatternA conflict or choice that repeats.
DevelopmentHow the later event raises the pressure or changes the meaning.
ComplicationA cost, exception, contradiction, or reversal.
ThemeA message broad enough to include the full pattern.
EvidenceDetails from at least two chapters.
Writing target: Avoid absolute claims such as “trust always wins.” Write a theme that accounts for both cooperation and rivalry.

Wrap-Up

Week 8 Quick Reference

The story so far

The same forces operate at every scale: loyalty draws people together, rivalry makes them withhold, and danger forces cooperation before trust is complete. The Australian alliance is neither false nor secure—it is useful, evidence-based, and already preparing for betrayal.

Keep asking

  • Which repeated conflict connects the chapters?
  • How does a later choice revise the meaning of an earlier one?
  • What complication prevents the theme from becoming simplistic?
