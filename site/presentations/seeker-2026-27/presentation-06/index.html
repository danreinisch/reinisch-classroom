Wrap-Up

Week 6 Quick Reference

The story so far

Kagan’s competence repeatedly saves lives and moves the mission forward, but every success exposes a complication. He claims cold logic, acts from compassion, demands trust, and secretly verifies it—creating a partnership that is real but unequal.

Keep asking

  • How do consequences revise a character’s beliefs?
  • When does secrecy protect the mission, and when does it weaken partnership?
  • Which action reveals more than the character’s own explanation?
