Day 4 • ELA Concept

Trace a Relationship Across Chapters

StageEvidence to include
Starting pointThe first impression, expectation, or power balance.
PressureThe new problem or information.
ResponseWhat the character says or chooses.
ChangeHow trust, responsibility, or judgment shifts.
MeaningWhat the change reveals about the character.
Writing target: Use chronological evidence to show development; do not describe the relationship as if it never changes.

Wrap-Up

Week 5 Quick Reference

The story so far

Preparation gives Kagan tools and confidence, but the jungle and Ella expose the limits of control. His first impressions do not disappear; they begin a relationship that survival pressure will repeatedly force him to revise.

Keep asking

  • Which judgments are evidence-based, and which are assumptions?
  • How does Ory support Kagan without replacing his responsibility?
  • When does compassion strengthen survival—and when does it complicate it?
