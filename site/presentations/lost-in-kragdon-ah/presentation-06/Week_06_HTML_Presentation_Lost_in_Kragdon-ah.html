Today's Grammar Focus: Fixing Fragments

Last week we learned to FIND fragments. Today we learn three ways to FIX them: add the missing subject, add the missing verb, or attach to a complete sentence.

Day 1: Three Ways to Fix a Fragment

Turning Fragments into Complete Sentences

How to Fix Sentence Fragments

METHOD 1: ADD THE MISSING SUBJECT If the fragment is missing WHO or WHAT, add it!
Fragment: "Swung his walking stick."
Fixed: "Tokin-ak swung his walking stick."
METHOD 2: ADD THE MISSING VERB If the fragment is missing the ACTION, add it!
Fragment: "The giant robber with the ax."
Fixed: "The giant robber with the ax charged at them."
METHOD 3: ATTACH TO A COMPLETE SENTENCE If it starts with "because," "when," "if," or "although," attach it!
Fragment: "Because he was trained by holy men."
Fixed: "Tokin-ak could fight because he was trained by holy men."

Day 1: Tokin-ak's Surprise

The Blind Man Who Fights Like a Warrior

"I am old, not dead." - Tokin-ak

"The old man whirled like a dervish and slammed the stick into the man's forehead."
  • The old man = Subject (WHO is doing the action?)
  • whirled = Verb (WHAT action is happening?)
  • Complete thought? YES! We know exactly what happened.

Why is this chapter called "A Whirling Dervish"?

A dervish is a person who spins wildly. Tokin-ak fought by spinning and whirling his stick so fast that no one could stop him. Everyone expected him to be helpless - they were WRONG!

Day 1: Tokin-ak's Backstory

How Did He Learn to Fight?

Born Blind in Velta-ah

Tokin-ak was born completely blind. His mother taught him to know every inch of their home and village. By age five, he could run and play with other children.

Given to the Holy Men

When holy men visited his village, they asked for a child to train. No family wanted to give up a healthy child, so the chief chose Tokin-ak. He left that day and never returned until now.

What the Holy Men Taught Him

The holy men lived in silence. While most people focus on speaking, they focused on listening. Tokin-ak learned to hear and sense things that others miss completely.

Day 2: Vocabulary Word 1

DERVISH

Definition: A person who whirls or spins wildly with frantic energy.

Understanding Dervish

WHAT IS IT? A dervish is someone who spins around very fast, often in a wild or frantic way. Originally, dervishes were members of religious orders known for spinning dances.
WHY IS IT IN THE STORY? Tokin-ak fought by spinning his stick so fast he was like a "whirling dervish." Everyone was shocked that the old blind man could move so quickly!
HOW DO I USE IT? "The dancer spun like a dervish, moving too fast to follow."

Day 2: Vocabulary Word 2

DEMURRED

Definition: Politely refused or expressed hesitation; declined to do something.

Understanding Demurred

WHAT IS IT? To demur means to politely say no or to hesitate before agreeing. It's a gentle way of refusing something.
WHY IS IT IN THE STORY? When Werda-ak asked Tokin-ak to teach him fighting moves, Tokin-ak demurred, saying "I am no teacher. I am only a novice myself."
HOW DO I USE IT? "When asked to take credit, she demurred, saying others deserved the praise."

Day 2: Vocabulary Word 3

INDEFATIGABLE

Definition: Unable to be tired out; having endless energy and stamina.

Understanding Indefatigable

WHAT IS IT? Indefatigable means you cannot be worn out or exhausted. The word has the prefix "in-" (not) + "fatigable" (able to be tired).
WHY IS IT IN THE STORY? The horses in Kragdon-ah are described as "indefatigable" - they can travel for hours at a steady pace without getting tired.
HOW DO I USE IT? "The indefatigable runner completed five marathons in five days."

Day 2: Vocabulary Words 4-6

SURVEYED

Definition: Looked carefully at something to examine it; studied an area thoroughly.

"Alex surveyed their surroundings, determining if it was safe to stop."

REVERED

Definition: Deeply respected and honored; treated with great admiration.

"The holy men are revered wherever they go."

LARDER

Definition: A place where food is stored; a food supply or pantry.

"Their larder was nearly bare after weeks of travel."

Day 2: Tricky Fragments

Long Phrases That LOOK Like Sentences

Don't Be Fooled!

Some fragments are LONG and LOOK like complete sentences. But length doesn't make a sentence complete!

Is This a Complete Sentence?

"The enormous dandra-ta with sharp spikes running down its curved spine and a long tail."

  • Subject: "The enormous dandra-ta" - YES!
  • Verb: NONE! What did the dandra-ta DO?
  • Complete thought: NO!

This is a FRAGMENT!

Fixed: "The enormous dandra-ta with sharp spikes running down its curved spine and a long tail attacked the village."

Day 2: More Tricky Fragments

Dependent Clauses That Sound Complete

Watch for These Signal Words

When a sentence starts with these words, make sure it finishes the thought:

because, when, if, although, while, since, after, before, unless

Fragment:

"When the twenty-two brave men went to hunt the creatures and never returned."

This sounds complete, but "when" makes it need more!

Fixed: "When the twenty-two brave men went to hunt the creatures and never returned, the village lost hope."

Fragment:

"Although Tokin-ak was blind and very old."

What about him? So what?

Fixed: "Although Tokin-ak was blind and very old, he fought like a young warrior."

"I already know the answer. It's because I didn't ask the right question." - Alex

Day 3: The Island Village

Matori-ah

What They See

From the hilltop, they see a beautiful sight: a sparkling blue lake with an island village called Matori-ah. Torches flicker in the darkness. It looks peaceful and well-protected.

The Island's Defenses

  • Surrounded by water - no land bridge
  • Rocky shores - large boulders make landing boats difficult
  • Single dock - easy to defend
  • Self-sufficient - they grow their own food

Why Tokin-ak Gets Special Treatment

When the barge master sees Tokin-ak's orange robe, he immediately helps them. Holy men are revered everywhere in Kragdon-ah!

Day 3: Chapter 19 - Dandra-ta

The Peaceful Village's Problem

DANDRA-TA

Definition: Terrifying creatures that have appeared on the island.

What Are Dandra-ta?

  • As tall as a man
  • Curved spine with spikes running down to a long tail
  • Scaly skin
  • Poisonous bite
  • They have killed livestock, animals, a child, and 22 hunters

The Village's Problem

The people of Matori-ah are peaceful farmers. They don't eat meat or believe in killing. They have never needed warriors - until now.

Day 3: Alex Makes a Deal

The Bargain

What Nanka-ak Wants:

For Alex and his team to kill the dandra-ta that are terrorizing the village.

What Alex Negotiates For:

  • Safe passage across the lake for all of them
  • Any weapons they have
  • Warm winter clothes
  • Extra blankets
  • As much food as they can carry

Why Alex Agrees

If they don't make this deal, they'll have to backtrack weeks of travel to go around the lake. Alex believes his team of trained warriors can succeed where 22 farmers failed.

Day 3: Jabril-ak's Help

The Shepherd's Information

Who is Jabril-ak?

Jabril-ak is Nanka-ak's son. He was the shepherd who watched a dandra-ta drag away one of their milk beasts. He feels guilty for falling asleep when it happened.

What Jabril-ak Shows Them

  • A windswept corner of the island
  • Huge rocks scattered around
  • Deep natural holes - perfect hiding places
  • The only part of the island without people around regularly

Alex's Plan

Alex decides to hunt the dandra-ta at night. The creatures have never been seen after dark, so they might have an advantage.

Day 3: Fragments in Context

Finding Fragments in Paragraphs

Find and Fix the Fragments

"Alex looked at the island. With torches flickering in the darkness. The village seemed peaceful. Because they had never needed warriors. The peaceful farmers of Matori-ah. They welcomed Tokin-ak with great respect."

Fixed Version

"Alex looked at the island with torches flickering in the darkness. The village seemed peaceful because they had never needed warriors. The peaceful farmers of Matori-ah had lived in safety for generations. They welcomed Tokin-ak with great respect."

3 fragments were fixed: Two were attached to nearby sentences, and one got a verb added.

Day 4: Writing Workshop

Putting It All Together

Today's Goals:

  • Review the three ways to fix fragments
  • Write a response using COMPLETE SENTENCES
  • Check your work for fragments before submitting
  • Use details from the story as evidence

Day 4: Writing Prompt

Your Prompt

Why does Alex agree to hunt the dandra-ta? Use details from Chapter 19 to explain his reasons.

Details to Include

  • What is blocking Alex's path?
  • What will Alex receive if he succeeds?
  • Why does Alex think his team can succeed where others failed?
  • What is Alex's ultimate mission?

Topic Sentence Starter

"Alex agrees to hunt the dandra-ta because it is the only way to continue his rescue mission."

Day 4: Writing Structure

Building Your Response

Remember Your Writing Structure

  • Topic Sentence: State what Alex agrees to and why
  • Supporting Detail 1: The lake is blocking his path
  • Supporting Detail 2: What Alex will receive (supplies, passage)
  • Supporting Detail 3: Why his team can succeed (trained warriors vs. farmers)
  • Conclusion: Restate the main idea in different words

Hints for Your Response

  • What would happen if Alex had to backtrack? (weeks of travel)
  • What makes his team different from the 22 men who failed?
  • What is driving all of Alex's decisions? (rescuing Lanta-eh)

Day 4: Writing Checklist

Check Your Work Before Submitting

Complete Sentence Checklist

  • Does every sentence have a SUBJECT? (Who or what?)
  • Does every sentence have a VERB? (Action or being?)
  • Does every sentence express a COMPLETE THOUGHT?
  • Did you check sentences starting with "Because," "When," or "If"?

Content Checklist

  • Do you have a topic sentence that answers the question?
  • Do you have at least three details from the story?
  • Do you have a conclusion sentence?

Day 4: Model Response

Example Answer

Alex agrees to hunt the dandra-ta because it is the only way to continue his rescue mission. First, a huge lake is blocking their path, and going around it would take weeks of extra travel. Second, if Alex kills the creatures, Nanka-ak will give them safe passage across the lake, plus weapons, food, and warm clothes for their journey. Third, Alex believes his team of trained warriors can succeed where the 22 peaceful farmers failed because his team knows how to fight dangerous enemies. Because saving Lanta-eh is more important than anything, Alex takes this dangerous risk to keep moving forward.

Notice:

Every sentence is complete with a subject, verb, and complete thought. The last sentence starts with "Because" but finishes the thought by attaching to "Alex takes this dangerous risk."
