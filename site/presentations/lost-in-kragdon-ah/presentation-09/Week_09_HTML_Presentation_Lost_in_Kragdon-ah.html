Today's ELA Focus: Context Clues

When you read an unknown word, LOOK AROUND IT! The words before and after often give hints about what it means.

Day 1: What Are Context Clues?

Clues Hidden in the Text

Understanding Context Clues

WHAT ARE CONTEXT CLUES? Context clues are hints found in the words, phrases, or sentences AROUND an unknown word that help you figure out its meaning.
WHY ARE THEY IMPORTANT? You can't always stop to look up every word. Context clues let you keep reading and still understand the text. They make you a faster, smarter reader!
HOW DO I FIND THEM? Read the sentence with the unknown word. Then read the sentences BEFORE and AFTER it. Look for: definitions, examples, synonyms, antonyms, or descriptions that give hints.

Day 1: Types of Context Clues

Five Ways Authors Give Hints

1. Definition Clue

The author directly tells you what the word means.

"The terrain, or type of land, was muddy."

2. Synonym Clue

A word with a similar meaning appears nearby.

"He was flummoxed, completely confused."

3. Antonym Clue

A word with the OPPOSITE meaning appears.

"Unlike her angry father, she seemed guileless and calm."

4. Example Clue

Examples help explain the word.

"Predators like bears, wolves, and lions..."

5. Inference Clue

You have to use logic and the overall situation to figure out the meaning. No direct hint is given - you have to think!

Day 1: Context Clues in Chapter 26

Real Examples from the Story

Example 1: ABATED

"As they dropped down, the constant freezing wind and temperatures that they had grown accustomed to in Hakun-ah abated. It wasn't toasty warm, but the clothes... were well able to keep them warm."

Context Clue: They were in freezing cold, then it got warmer (not freezing anymore). ABATED means lessened or stopped.

Example 2: FLUMMOXED

"Alex was flummoxed. He didn't want a bear for a dinner guest, but he also didn't know what to do about its apparently mellow mood."

Context Clue: Alex didn't know what to do - he was unsure. FLUMMOXED means confused or bewildered.

Context Clue Practice

"At first, he had done it because he had given an oath, but his heart wasn't in it. He was resentful."

What does RESENTFUL mean based on context? (Hint: His heart wasn't in it - he didn't want to do it.)

Answer: Bitter, annoyed, or unhappy about being forced to do something.

Day 2: What Are Word Parts?

Prefixes, Roots, and Suffixes

Understanding Word Parts

WHAT ARE WORD PARTS? Words can be broken into smaller pieces: PREFIX (beginning), ROOT (middle/main meaning), and SUFFIX (ending). Each part adds meaning!
WHY ARE THEY IMPORTANT? If you know common prefixes, roots, and suffixes, you can figure out thousands of words you've never seen before! It's like having a secret code.
HOW DO I USE THEM? Break the word apart. Identify each piece. Add the meanings together to understand the whole word.

Day 2: Common Prefixes

Word Beginnings That Change Meaning

Important Prefixes to Know

Prefix Meaning Example
UN- not, opposite of uncomfortable = NOT comfortable
RE- again, back revitalized = made alive AGAIN
PRE- before predecessors = those who came BEFORE
IM-/IN- not impatient = NOT patient
DIS- not, opposite of disappeared = NOT there anymore

Day 2: Common Suffixes

Word Endings That Change Meaning

Important Suffixes to Know

Suffix Meaning Example
-LESS without guileless = WITHOUT guile (trickery)
-FUL full of resentful = FULL OF resentment
-ABLE/-IBLE can be done defendable = CAN BE defended
-LY in a way that casually = IN A casual WAY
-NESS state of being darkness = STATE OF BEING dark

Day 2: Breaking Down Words

Practice with Story Words

Word: UNCOMFORTABLE

UN + COMFORT + ABLE

UN = not | COMFORT = ease/relaxation | ABLE = able to be

Meaning: NOT able to be at ease = feeling awkward or uneasy

Word: REVITALIZED

RE + VITAL + IZED

RE = again | VITAL = life/living | IZED = made into

Meaning: Made alive AGAIN = given new energy and strength

Day 2: Vocabulary Word 1

TERRAIN

Definition: The physical features of an area of land (flat, hilly, rocky, muddy, etc.).

Understanding Terrain

WHAT IS IT? Terrain describes what the land looks like and how it feels to travel across. Mountains have rough terrain; plains have flat terrain.
WHY IS IT IN THE STORY? The trail down the mountain was steep and muddy - this was "terrain these horses were built for." Their sturdy horses handled the difficult land.
WORD PARTS: From Latin "terra" meaning EARTH or LAND. You see this root in: territory, terrace, terrestrial.

Day 2: Vocabulary Words 2-4

DESOLATION

Definition: A state of emptiness and lifelessness; a barren, lonely place.

Word Parts: DESOL (lonely, alone) + ATION (state of) = state of being empty/alone

"Aside from the few buildings, Alex saw nothing but desolation."

GUILELESS

Definition: Innocent and honest; without trickery or deceit.

Word Parts: GUILE (trickery) + LESS (without) = without trickery = honest

"Versa-eh's guileless eyes convinced him of her intentions."

CONTRITE

Definition: Feeling sorry for wrongdoing; remorseful.

"Werda-ak was contrite. 'Thank you for choosing me to come with you.'"

Day 2: Vocabulary Words 5-6

STAMINA

Definition: The ability to keep doing something for a long time; lasting energy and strength.

"These beautiful horses will not have the stamina that our old horses did."

MALLEABLE

Definition: Easily shaped, influenced, or controlled.

"Don't assume that because she is young, she is malleable or dumb."

Combining Strategies

The best readers use BOTH context clues AND word parts together! If one strategy doesn't give you enough information, try the other.

UN + COMFORT + ABLE

Day 4: Writing Workshop

Putting It All Together

Today's Goals:

  • Write a response about the deal Alex made with the trader
  • Use vocabulary words from this week
  • Practice clear paragraph structure

Day 4: Writing Prompt

Your Prompt

What deal did Alex make with the trader, and why was it a good decision for the team?

Details to Include

  • What did the trader offer?
  • What did Alex have to do in return?
  • What danger came from the deal?
  • Why was it still worth it?

Topic Sentence Starter

"Alex made a deal with the trader to exchange their mountain horses for fast thoroughbreds if he would take the trader's daughter Versa-eh to Grinta-ah."

Day 4: Writing Structure

Building Your Response

Remember Your Writing Structure

  • Topic Sentence: State what the deal was
  • Supporting Detail 1: Describe the horses they received (beautiful thoroughbreds vs. their sturdy mountain horses)
  • Supporting Detail 2: Explain the danger (Rundi-ak's ambush)
  • Supporting Detail 3: Describe why it was worth it (faster travel, Versa-eh actually wanted to go)
  • Conclusion: Sum up why this was a good decision

Try Using These Vocabulary Words!

terrain, stamina, guileless, contrite, desolation, malleable

Day 4: Writing Checklist

Check Your Work Before Submitting

Content Checklist

  • Do you have a topic sentence that answers the question?
  • Do you have at least three details from the story?
  • Do you have a conclusion sentence?
  • Did you explain WHY the deal was good?

Grammar Reminders

  • Use past tense consistently (events already happened)
  • Check subject-verb agreement
  • Use complete sentences with capital letters and periods

Day 4: Model Response

Example Answer

Alex made a deal with the trader to exchange their sturdy mountain horses for beautiful fast thoroughbreds in return for taking the trader's daughter Versa-eh to Grinta-ah. First, the new horses were magnificent - unlike their old horses built for rough terrain, these thoroughbreds could cover much more ground quickly, even if they had less stamina for long journeys. Second, the deal did bring danger when Rundi-ak ambushed them, trying to stop Versa-eh from leaving. However, Senta-eh and Monda-ak quickly defeated the attackers. Third, the deal turned out even better than expected because Versa-eh actually wanted to leave - she was not the guileless, malleable girl her father thought she was, but a clever young woman with her own plans. The trade was a good decision because the team gained faster horses for the journey ahead without having to go out of their way.
