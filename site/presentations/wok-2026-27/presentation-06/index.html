Day 2 • ELA Concept

Demonstration Before Destruction

The cannon fires wide of the caves.

Mandran shows capability without immediately killing captives he values.

Alex’s former home is struck.

The damage is personal, visible, and symbolic.

Everyone watches the new stama.

Fear and cultural belief make the weapon a public event.

Sekun-ak answers theatrically.

Defiance reframes the cannonball as something the tribe can reject.

Today’s bridge: A demonstration targets belief as much as buildings.

The Target Reveals the Strategy

Shots repeatedly strike the waterfall.

Mandran aims to remove water, not merely frighten the tribe.

Captives are valuable to the invaders.

Destruction is calibrated to force surrender while preserving labor.

The portal sits behind the overhang.

The same target threatens Alex’s route home.

Containers and a cave basin are filled.

The community converts remaining time into survival preparation.

Today’s bridge: The meaning of an attack becomes clear when readers identify what the attacker wants to remove.
