Wrap-Up

Week 11 Quick Reference

The story so far

Challenge traditions reveal unequal power, courage requires adaptation, and healing reveals the community’s values. By the return to Winten-ah, home is defined through care, responsibility, welcome, and shared rebuilding.

Keep asking

  • Who should consent when a whole community carries the risk?
  • How does courage depend on adaptation?
  • What practical actions create belonging?
