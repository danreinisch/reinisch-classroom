What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Conflict, perspective, and moral choice. We trace how time pressure, environmental danger, accidents, and rescue decisions test a team’s responsibility to one another.

I can…

  • Explain how setting creates conflict and suspense.
  • Compare different responses to danger and delay.
  • Develop a theme about perseverance and shared responsibility.

Wrap-Up

Week 13 Quick Reference

The story so far

Perseverance is not simple forward motion. The team weighs time against safety, adapts to accumulating setbacks, rescues one another, and accepts delay to care for the person who carried them to safety.

Keep asking

  • When does urgency justify greater risk?
  • How do several small problems create suspense?
  • Why must rescue include aftercare?
