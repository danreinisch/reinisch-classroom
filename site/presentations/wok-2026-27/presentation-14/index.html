Wrap-Up

Week 14 Quick Reference

The story so far

The third Thundan is shaped by care, consent, testimony, and unequal preparation. Moral leadership means waiting for a teammate, earning trust, and choosing the person best suited to act.

Keep asking

  • When should urgency yield to care?
  • What evidence supports informed consent?
  • Can a contest be rule-following but still unequal?
