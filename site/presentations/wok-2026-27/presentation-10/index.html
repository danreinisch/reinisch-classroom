What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Perspective, culture, and ethical leadership. We compare how the same victory is understood by Tinta-ak, Mandran, Alex, the tribes, and the defeated champion.

I can…

  • Compare perspectives on victory and responsibility.
  • Explain how cultural expectations influence choices.
  • Evaluate what ethical leadership requires after conflict.

Wrap-Up

Week 10 Quick Reference

The story so far

Victory reveals values through what happens next. Alex and the tribes choose healing, belonging, alliance, and careful use of new infrastructure, while Mandran’s response exposes leadership based on control and usefulness.

Keep asking

  • How should an ethical leader treat a defeated opponent?
  • Does safety create obligations toward allies?
  • Can communities use technology without accepting the values of its builders?
