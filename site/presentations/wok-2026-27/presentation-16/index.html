Our reading skill

Theme and synthesis. We connect a new threat, a friendship-based rescue, and a strategically prepared rematch to develop a larger claim about trust and responsibility.

  • Connect events across the novel to a shared theme.
  • Compare friendship with formal cultural rules.
  • Organize evidence into a clear thematic claim.

Choose Your Level

Foundation — 4–6 sentences

Use one detail and frames: “One important event is ___.” “This changes ___ because ___.” “This shows ___.”

Supported — one developed paragraph

Use two details from different chapters and explain how both support one claim.
