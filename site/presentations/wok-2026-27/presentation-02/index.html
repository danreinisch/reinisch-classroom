Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Evidence-based leadership analysis. We compare how characters protect others, gather information, cooperate, and adjust plans as danger grows.

I can…

  • Use actions and consequences as evidence about leadership.
  • Compare protective and strategic responses to danger.
  • Explain how cooperation changes what a small group can accomplish.

Leadership Assigns Roles

Alex

Reads the threat, chooses concealment, and coordinates the counterattack.

Sanda

Uses the bow from a protected position and stops a shooter.

Amy

Acts as Sanda’s observer because close combat would expose her.

Wenta-eh and Monda-ak

Close the distance where the invaders’ guns are less useful.

Today’s bridge: A role is effective when it matches a person’s skill, the terrain, and the immediate threat.

Week 2 Quick Reference

The story so far

The growing threat forces leadership to change pace. Alex protects through role assignment, follows through deliberate patience, and pauses for reconnaissance before attempting a rescue.

Keep asking

  • How does strategy differ from hesitation?
  • When should a leader accept help from people they want to protect?
  • Why does accurate information matter before action?
