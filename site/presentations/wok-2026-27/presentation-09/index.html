Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Apply problem-solving analysis. We evaluate how new information, cultural rules, specialized skills, and collective decision making create a narrow alternative to defeat.

I can…

  • Explain how information changes the available choices.
  • Evaluate the risks and benefits of a cultural challenge.
  • Analyze how a team is selected for uncertain problems.

Week 9 Quick Reference

The story so far

The balance changes through information rather than greater force. Reggie reveals a binding cultural convention, leaders accept a narrow risk for continuity, and a carefully designed team gives the tribes a chance.

Keep asking

  • What can information accomplish that strength cannot?
  • How can a cultural rule limit power?
  • Should uncertain teams emphasize variety or specialization?
