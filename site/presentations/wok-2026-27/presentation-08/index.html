Our reading skill

Day 1 • ELA Concept

A Double Threat Creates One Opening

The pursuit group closes from behind.

Stopping on the trail is not possible.

Godat-ta approaches from ahead.

Direct escape is blocked.

Alex disturbs plants farther forward.

The trail suggests the group continued.

Everyone hides off the path.

The two threats meet while the group remains concealed.

Today’s bridge: Problem solving often begins by identifying how two pressures interact—not treating each one separately.
