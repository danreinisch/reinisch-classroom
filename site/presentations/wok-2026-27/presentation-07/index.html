What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Setting, community, and problem solving. We examine how a damaged home, a hidden exit, and a pursuing army shape choices about survival and responsibility.

I can…

  • Explain how setting creates problems and limits choices.
  • Trace how a community organizes an evacuation.
  • Evaluate how individual risks affect collective survival.

Setting Creates the Central Problem

The waterfall is rubble.

The caves cannot support the tribes for long.

Smoke signals appear.

Possible allies create a destination beyond the siege.

The ceiling tunnel is narrow.

Escape requires labor, timing, and an organized order.

The enemy celebrates below.

Their confidence gives the defenders a brief planning window.

Today’s bridge: Setting is not background when its resources, barriers, and risks determine what characters must do.
