What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Structure, conflict, and consequences. We trace how a scouting choice accelerates into pursuit, isolation, and a confrontation that leaves Alex without an easy plan.

I can…

  • Identify the event that accelerates a conflict.
  • Trace consequences through a pursuit sequence.
  • Explain how narrowed choices develop theme and character.
