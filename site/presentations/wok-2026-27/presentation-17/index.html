Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop whole-book synthesis. We connect the rematch, its consequences, Garta-ah’s liberation, and Darand’s exile to evaluate what freedom and justice require after conflict.

I can…

  • Trace a theme across several connected outcomes.
  • Compare justice, retaliation, mercy, and exile.
  • Use evidence from different chapters to develop a coherent claim.

Week 17 Quick Reference

The story so far

The rematch resolves the immediate conflict but opens harder questions. Freedom requires rule-based restraint, recovery reclaims community space, and justice is tested when an exiled former enemy needs mercy.

Keep asking

  • How can precedent shape fairness?
  • Why does legitimate leadership matter after victory?
  • Can mercy protect safety through conditions?
