Day 1 • ELA Concept

Crisis Cycles: Adapt, Stabilize, Repeat

Problem

Fire consumes breathable air.

Adaptation

Open a small gap and fight the spiders entering.

Stabilize

Rebuild fire and recover until oxygen drops again.

Repeat

Continue the cycle until the cloud passes.

Today’s bridge: Survival is repeated observation, adjustment, cooperation, and endurance.

Day 3 • ELA Concept

Foreshadowing Inside Normal Life

Signals of stability

Cabin, krinta, shared work, stories, love, and routine.

Signals of danger

Every mother dies; Senta-eh is ill; Niten-eh examines her abdomen.

Final clue

A red rash appears—the known sign of sickness.

Effect

Recovery is real but not permanent or complete.

Today’s bridge: Quiet pacing makes every medical clue louder.
