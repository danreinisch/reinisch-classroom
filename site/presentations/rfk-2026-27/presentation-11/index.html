Choose Your Level

Foundation — 4–6 sentences

Use one detail and frames: “One important event is ___.” “This changes ___ because ___.” “This shows ___.”

Supported — one developed paragraph

Use two details from different chapters and explain how both support one claim.

Wrap-Up

Week 11 Quick Reference

The story so far

The crisis tests several leadership models. Alex’s targeted risk creates an opening, Harta-ak’s diplomacy ends the invasion, and the return to Prata-ah reframes leadership as patient cooperation.

Keep asking

  • What makes a leadership response effective?
  • Why does offering an exit strengthen a threat?
  • How can cooperation accomplish what individual heroism cannot?
