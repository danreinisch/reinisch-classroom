Day 1 • ELA Concept

Foreshadowing: Small Sign, Large Promise

Normal pattern

Friends leave, homes are built, hunting continues, and Alex naps.

Pattern break

A large spider floats from the sky and leaps at faces.

Character signal

Senta-eh’s rare fear tells readers the threat is severe.

Reader question

If one creature causes dread, what will many do?

Today’s bridge: The first zisla-ta is manageable; Senta-eh’s reaction reveals the real scale.

Day 3 • ELA Concept

Crisis = Threat + Constraint + Choice

Threat

Millions of airborne spiders overwhelm by number.

Constraint

The cave must be blocked, but people need air and wood is limited.

Choice

The community assigns shelters, protects children, and feeds the fire.

Escalation

Noise, smell, fuel use, and breathing create new problems.

Today’s bridge: Every solution changes the setting and may create another challenge.
