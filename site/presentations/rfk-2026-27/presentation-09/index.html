Our reading skill

Crisis, character, and theme. We trace how Alex responds to a problem he cannot solve and how the search changes what hope means.

  • Use setting and discovered evidence to develop a theme.

A Crisis No Sword Can Solve

A visible warning

Alex notices that Senta-eh has the same rash that preceded earlier childbirth deaths.

A painful truth

Senta-eh and Lanta-eh already know the likely outcome; Alex refuses to stop searching.

A possible clue

Lanta-eh identifies a hidden place connected to other people from Alex’s time.

A new kind of conflict

Alex can act with courage, but effort cannot guarantee the result he wants.

Wrap-Up

Week 9 Quick Reference

The story so far

The search confirms that other modern people reached Kragdon-ah, but it cannot give Alex control over Senta-eh’s fate. The diary transforms evidence into a warning about blame and irreversible consequences.

Keep asking

  • How can hope exist without certainty?
  • When does responsibility become a false need for control?
  • Why can evidence matter even when it disappoints?
