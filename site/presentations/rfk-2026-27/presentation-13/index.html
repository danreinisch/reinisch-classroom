Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Revelation and reinterpretation. We examine how visitors, technology, and future history reframe earlier mysteries without erasing personal loss.

I can…

  • Explain how new information changes the meaning of earlier events.
  • Distinguish what a revelation explains from what it cannot undo.
  • Connect historical exposition to theme and character perspective.

Week 13 Quick Reference

The story so far

The visitors connect Kragdon-ah’s mysteries to humanity’s distant future. Their history explains the portals and the childbirth crisis, while Lanta-eh’s final revelation transforms her sacrifice into the precise message that makes repair possible.
