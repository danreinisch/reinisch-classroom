Justice, Revenge, or Mercy?

IdeaWorking definition
RevengePain answered with pain, often driven by anger and personal loss.
JusticeAccountability tied to actions and community protection.
MercyNot using the full punishment or power available.
ComplicationOne choice can contain more than one motive.

Day 3 • ELA Concept

Resolution by Transformation

Return them?

Draka-ak refuses to save them and promises another attack.

Kill them?

Tradition allows it, but the men are now known as individuals.

Keep them?

Permanent confinement drains resources and denies a future.

Transform

They disavow Lasta-ah and freely join a new community.

Today’s bridge: A durable resolution changes relationships and incentives—not only who wins.
