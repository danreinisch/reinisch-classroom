Wrap-Up

Week 10 Quick Reference

The story so far

The novel pairs Senta-eh’s death with the legacy she deliberately creates, jumps three years to reveal Alex’s changed life, then uses a new threat to call him back into public leadership.

Keep asking

  • How can structure show the long-term effect of loss?
  • What makes a legacy active rather than symbolic?
  • How do private and public responsibilities compete?
