What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Choice, perspective, and theme. We evaluate the responsibilities, loyalties, and ideas of home that pull Alex toward two different worlds.

I can…

  • Identify responsibilities competing within one decision.
  • Explain how perspective shapes the meaning of home.
  • Build a theme claim from choices and consequences.
