MoveQuestion to ask

Day 2 • ELA Concept

Read the Encounter Twice

Observable evidence

O’Brien mentions the dictionary, speaks privately, and gives an address.

Winston’s interpretation

The contact confirms the secret understanding he has imagined.

Alternative reading

An Inner Party member may have other reasons to create private contact.

Narrative implication

Winston recognizes the path may end in capture and still accepts it.

Today’s bridge: Point-of-view analysis asks what the focal character makes of details—and what readers can still question beyond that interpretation.

Argument partTextual content

Wrap-Up

Week 7 Quick Reference

The story so far

Winston and Julia share rebellion but build it on different assumptions. Syme’s disappearance, O’Brien’s invitation, and Winston’s family memories become evidence that each interprets through hope, fear, and experience. Their strongest shared claim—that inner loyalty can survive—is still untested.

Keep asking

  • What is observed, and what is inferred?
  • Which assumptions make a claim persuasive to a character?
  • How can we evaluate a hopeful claim without mocking or accepting it automatically?
