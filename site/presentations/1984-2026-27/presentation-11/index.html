What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Develop language and tone analysis. We examine the rhetoric of coercion without dwelling on graphic detail, focusing on how questions, repetition, roles, and sentence control reshape reality.

I can…

  • Analyze how repetition and dialogue structure create pressure.
  • Explain how O’Brien controls both the question and the acceptable answer.
  • Distinguish genuine inquiry from language designed to force agreement.

Wrap-Up

Week 11 Quick Reference

The story so far

Room 101 first becomes powerful as an unexplained repeated threat. O’Brien then controls question, answer, consequence, and relief while naming himself teacher and healer. The four/five exchange reveals the full target: not merely Winston’s speech, but his standard for knowing reality.

Keep asking

  • Who controls the definitions in this exchange?
  • Is the question seeking information or enforcing an answer?
  • How does repetition reshape meaning rather than merely repeat it?
