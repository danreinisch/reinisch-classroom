Wrap-Up

Week 5 Quick Reference

The story so far

Part One closes with Winston imagining refuge and expecting capture. Part Two begins by reversing his interpretation of Julia, then slows the movement from message to meeting so connection itself becomes a carefully planned act of rebellion.

Keep asking

  • Which expectations belong to Winston, and which does the text confirm?
  • How can a setting create both exposure and cover?
  • What changes when private feeling becomes planned action?
