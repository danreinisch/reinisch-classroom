Skill Focus

What We Are Learning

Our reading skill

Synthesize theme and transformation. We trace Winston’s beliefs across the novel, distinguish coerced action from inner change, and analyze how recurring symbols and relationships acquire final meanings.

I can…

  • Trace a character belief from earlier evidence to later transformation.
  • Explain how Room 101 targets an individualized fear without sensationalizing the scene.
  • Synthesize betrayal, love, identity, and power into a coherent thematic interpretation.

Wrap-Up

Week 13 Quick Reference

The story so far

Winston’s recovery leaves one hidden loyalty intact, so Room 101 targets the fear capable of reversing it. His demand that Julia take his place fulfills the novel’s deepest definition of betrayal. Their café reunion then shows the consequence: words remain, but intimacy, trust, and shared resistance do not.

Keep asking

  • Which part of Winston changes—behavior, belief, loyalty, or identity?
  • How does a recurring setting make transformation visible?
  • Does my theme statement explain a pattern rather than summarize an event?
