Shared Defiance, Different Viewpoints

Winston

Wants historical truth and imagines organized change.

Julia

Distrusts Party rules and breaks them when they block private desire.

Shared ground

Both reject the Party’s ownership of inner life and loyalty.

Tension

They do not assign the same importance to history, theory, or collective rebellion.

Today’s bridge: A relationship can create resistance without erasing difference. Point-by-point comparison prevents “they both rebel” from becoming an oversimplification.

Wrap-Up

Week 6 Quick Reference

The story so far

The Golden Country gives Winston’s imagined freedom a real landscape. His relationship with Julia turns private loyalty into resistance, and the rented room gives that resistance a home. Yet the paperweight’s glass and the room’s warning signs keep fragility inside the dream.

Keep asking

  • When does privacy become a form of resistance?
  • How do symbols change as their contexts change?
  • Which warnings do the characters notice—and which do they explain away?
