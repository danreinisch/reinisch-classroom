O’Brien’s Argument Map

Claim

The Party seeks power entirely for its own sake.

Definition

Power means power over human beings—body and mind.

Reasoning

Controlling perception and suffering demonstrates ownership of reality.

Stance

Absolute, contemptuous of humanitarian justification, certain of victory.

Today’s bridge: A speaker’s candor can make a claim feel credible, but directness is still not evidence. Evaluate reasoning and support separately from tone.

Wrap-Up

Week 12 Quick Reference

The story so far

O’Brien renames destruction as cure, states that power is its own purpose, and presents a future built on controlled suffering. His calm certainty, parallel syntax, and dehumanizing imagery make the vision feel planned and total, while the mirror shows its consequence on one human body.

Keep asking

  • Does directness make a claim true, or only clear?
  • Which words reverse ordinary moral categories?
  • How does one image carry an entire political claim?
