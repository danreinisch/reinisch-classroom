Recipe 1: Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich
Makes: 1 sandwich
Ingredients
  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon jelly
Steps
  1. Lay two slices of bread flat on a plate.
  2. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread.
  3. Spread jelly on the other slice of bread.
  4. Press both slices together.
  5. Cut in half and enjoy!
Recipe 2: Bowl of Cereal
Makes: 1 bowl
Ingredients
  • 1 cup cereal
  • ½ cup cold milk
Steps
  1. Get a bowl from the cabinet.
  2. Pour 1 cup of cereal into the bowl.
  3. Pour ½ cup of cold milk over the cereal.
  4. Add a spoon and enjoy!
Recipe 3: Fruit Salad
Makes: 2 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • ½ cup blueberries
  • ½ cup grapes
  • 1 teaspoon honey
Steps
  1. Wash all the fruit under cold water.
  2. Cut the strawberries in half.
  3. Measure ½ cup of blueberries and add to bowl.
  4. Measure ½ cup of grapes and add to bowl.
  5. Combine all fruit in a large bowl.
  6. Drizzle 1 teaspoon of honey on top.
  7. Stir gently and serve.
