Recipe Cards
PB&J Sandwich
Serves: 1 sandwich
Ingredients
  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tablespoons of peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon of jelly
Steps
  • Lay both slices of bread flat.
  • Spread peanut butter on one slice.
  • Spread jelly on the other slice.
  • Press the two slices together.
  • Cut in half and enjoy!
Bowl of Cereal
Serves: 1 bowl
Ingredients
  • 1 cup of cereal
  • 1 cup of milk
  • Optional: 1 banana
Steps
  • Get a bowl from the cabinet.
  • Pour cereal into the bowl.
  • Pour milk over the cereal.
  • Optional: slice the banana and add on top.
  • Eat with a spoon.
Fruit Salad
Serves: 2 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 apple
  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup of grapes
  • 1 cup of strawberries
  • 1 tablespoon of honey
Steps
  • Wash all the fruit.
  • Cut the apple into small pieces.
  • Slice the banana.
  • Cut the strawberries in half.
  • Mix all fruit in a bowl.
  • Drizzle honey on top.
