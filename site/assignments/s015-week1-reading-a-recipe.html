Recipe 1: Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich
Makes: 1 sandwich
Ingredients
  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon jelly
Steps
  1. Lay two slices of bread flat on a plate.
  2. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread.
  3. Spread jelly on the other slice of bread.
  4. Press both slices together.
  5. Cut in half and enjoy!
Recipe 2: Bowl of Cereal
Makes: 1 bowl
Ingredients
  • 1 cup cereal
  • ¾ cup milk
Steps
  1. Pour cereal into a bowl.
  2. Pour milk over the cereal.
  3. Eat right away before it gets soggy!
Recipe 3: Fruit Salad
Makes: 2 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup strawberries, sliced
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • ½ cup blueberries
  • 1 tablespoon honey
Steps
  1. Wash all the fruit under cold water.
  2. Slice the strawberries and banana.
  3. Combine all fruit in a bowl.
  4. Drizzle honey on top and stir gently.
  5. Serve immediately or refrigerate.
