Recipe 1: Scrambled Eggs
Makes: 1 serving
Ingredients
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Steps
  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Whisk the eggs until mixed.
  3. Melt butter in a pan over low heat.
  4. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  5. Stir gently until eggs are cooked through.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
Recipe 2: Banana Smoothie
Makes: 1 serving
Ingredients
  • 1 banana
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon honey
Steps
  1. Peel and slice the banana.
  2. Put all ingredients into the blender.
  3. Blend for 30 seconds until smooth.
  4. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
Recipe 3: Grilled Cheese Sandwich
Makes: 1 sandwich
Ingredients
  • 2 slices bread
  • 2 slices cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
Steps
  1. Spread butter on one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place cheese between the bread slices.
  3. Heat a pan on medium heat.
  4. Cook sandwich 3 minutes per side until golden brown.
