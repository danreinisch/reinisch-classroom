For a fraction, leave Whole blank. For −2 1/3, enter Whole = −2, Numerator = 1, Denominator = 3. The minus sign applies to the entire mixed number. Its fractional part must be nonnegative and less than 1.

Enter integers in the boxes, without decimals or commas. Each input can be from −1,000,000,000 to 1,000,000,000; denominators cannot be zero. To enter a whole number by itself, use it as the numerator with denominator 1.
