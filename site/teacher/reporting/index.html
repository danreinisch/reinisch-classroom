📋 Generate Quarterly Progress Reports

Generate printable quarterly progress reports for all active students on your caseload. Reports include goal descriptions, baselines, targets, progress data, and narrative summaries.

Note: This will generate a report for all active students with goals. The report will open in a new window and can be printed or saved as PDF. Each student's section will start on a new page for easy printing.
