    Quick rules & controls

    Checkmate ends the game; the king is never captured. Castling, en passant, and all four promotion choices are supported.

    These are casual, untimed games. Three repetitions of a position or 50 moves by each side without a pawn move or capture automatically end in a draw. Tutorial and challenge positions are separate from your saved games.

    Learning plays random legal moves. Friendly looks a little ahead. Challenge looks further, with a short thinking limit. These levels do not have chess ratings.

    Take your time. You can ask for a hint.
